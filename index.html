Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

AppDealer is built with user privacy as a fundamental principle:

  • No Data Collection: AppDealer does not collect, track, store, or transmit any personal data, analytics, or usage metrics.
  • Screen Recording Permission: If granted, Screen Recording access is used strictly on-device in real-time to generate visual window previews for running applications. No screenshots, window content, or audio are ever saved to disk permanently or transmitted over any network.
  • No Third-Party SDKs: AppDealer contains no third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics frameworks.

If you have any questions regarding this policy, please contact us at appdealer.support@gmail.com.
